Tóm tắt

This paper presents a comprehensive survey of deep learning approaches applied to Vietnamese Natural Language Processing (NLP). We systematically review over 200 research papers published between 2018 and 2024, covering tasks such as word segmentation, part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, and machine translation. Our analysis reveals significant advances driven by transformer-based architectures adapted for Vietnamese linguistic characteristics, including tone marks and morphological complexity. We identify key challenges and future research directions specific to Vietnamese NLP.
